WidgetDeck on your iPhone

  1. Open this workspace in Safari over HTTPS.
  2. Open the Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. If offered, leave Open as Web App enabled, then tap Add.
  4. Open WidgetDeck from your Home Screen and sign in. On your personal phone, choose Keep me signed in if you want to stay signed in after closing it.

Your account is still required to access your tools. Connect to the internet to load and save your work.
